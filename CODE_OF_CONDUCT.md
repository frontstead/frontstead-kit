# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Frontstead Kit contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, professional, and harassment-free community. We welcome participation regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

Expected behavior includes:

- Giving specific, constructive feedback about ideas and code.
- Respecting differing experiences and technical choices.
- Accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Protecting private, customer, and MLS information.
- Focusing discussions on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sharing private information without permission, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents Frontstead Kit.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer. If that is unsafe or the concern involves a maintainer, use [GitHub's report-abuse process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
